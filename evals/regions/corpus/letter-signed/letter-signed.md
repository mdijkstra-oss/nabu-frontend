Dear Sir,

I am writing about the inspection of the roof void at 14 Bellwether Row, which
your office carried out on 3 March.

Your surveyor arrived without notice. At 13:00 we opened the roof void together,
and he spent perhaps ten minutes in it. At 13:20 he came down and said the
timbers were sound. He did not take photographs and he did not measure anything.

I have since had a second opinion. The timbers are not sound. Two of them have
rot at the wall plate, and the felt has failed along most of the north slope.

I would like the inspection carried out again, by somebody else, and I would
like to be told in advance when that will be.

I have kept the earlier correspondence and can send copies if that helps.

Yours faithfully,

F. Hanley
