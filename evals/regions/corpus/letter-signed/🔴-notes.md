# letter-signed

Letter. Signed at the end, dated in the body with the year only in the ref line, two times inside.

- Person 🟢 — `f hanley` 0–18. The signature at the end owns the whole letter, reaching backwards.
- Date 🔴 — one region this run.

Known gaps:

- `13:00` and `13:20` were found on an earlier run and vanished on this one. Same input, same prompt. Variance, not a rule problem.
- When they are found, their regions run to the end of the letter, because the partition rule gives a date everything up to the next one. Containment fixes that.
