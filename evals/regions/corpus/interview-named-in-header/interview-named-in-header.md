# Interview with Ms. Okafor, 3 March 2026

Conducted at the site office. Recording begins mid-sentence.

Q: When did you first notice the damp?

A: Around the middle of February. It was on the ceiling of the back bedroom,
and at that point it was only a stain.

Q: Did you report it?

A: I called the office the same week. Nobody came.

Q: And after that?

A: I called again in March. By then the plaster had started to come away, and
I was keeping a bucket under it.

Q: Who did you speak to?

A: I never got a name. It was a different person each time.

Q: Was any of this put in writing?

A: I wrote once, at the end of March. I never had a reply to that either.

Q: Is the bedroom in use now?

A: No. My son sleeps in the front room.
