# Bellwether Row: position at 20 March 2026

## Where we are

The roof works are held pending the revised survey. Two properties were left
out of the February survey and were added to the schedule on 7 March. Nothing
can be ordered until the revisions come back.

## What the residents are saying

We held a short session on 12 March. It was not a formal meeting and no minutes
were taken, but the recording gives the sense of it.

Mrs. Sharma: I have been asking about the door since January and it has been
fixed once, badly.

Bell: The bins are the same story.

Mrs. Sharma: I do not want another letter. I want somebody to come and look.

Ms. Okafor was not able to attend and sent her comments in writing. She repeated
the point about the survey and asked for a date.

## Costs

The contractor's revised figure arrived at 16:40 on 18 March, after the meeting
had been arranged, so it was not discussed. It is roughly nine per cent above
the figure we took to committee on 3 March.

## Next
