# note-nested-dates

Note. Three levels of date: the note's own date, an earlier date discussed inside it, and a time inside that.

- Person 🟢 — empty. The writer is never named and the duty staff and caretaker own nothing.
- Date 🟡 — two regions, values right, including 31 December resolved back to 2025.

Known gaps:

- The note's date owns only 0–2. It should own the whole note, with 31 December nested inside it.
- `10:00` and `09:00` produce nothing. After the change they should resolve from the innermost enclosing region — 10:00 to 31 December, 09:00 to 16 January.
- This file is the one that proves "most recent date" is the wrong fill rule.
