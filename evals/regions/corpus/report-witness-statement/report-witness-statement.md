# Statement of J. Hanley

Taken at the depot office. The witness read the statement back and signed it.

I have worked at the depot since 2019 and I am usually on the gate.

On the day in question I opened up as normal. The delivery was not on the
sheet, which is why I remember it. I let it in because the driver said it had
been cleared by the office and I had no reason to doubt him.

Mr. Bell came out about an hour later and asked who had authorised it. I told
him nobody had asked me to authorise anything. He said he would look into it
and went back inside.

I did not see the delivery leave. He never came back to me about it, and as far
as I know nothing was ever written down.

I have read this statement and it is true.
