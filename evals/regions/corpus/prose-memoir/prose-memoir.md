# The best day of my life

I have told this story often enough that I no longer trust my own version of it,
so what follows is the version I have settled on.

We drove out to the coast in my brother's car, which had no heater and a door
that would not shut properly. He complained about the road the whole way and I
let him. My mother had packed food for four people and there were two of us.

The weather turned somewhere after the bridge. By the time we parked, the rain
was coming sideways off the water and the car park was empty except for a van
with its engine running.

We ate in the car. That was the whole of it. My brother said afterwards that it
had been a waste of a day, and he was wrong, and I have never been able to
explain to him why.

I went back the following spring on my own. The van was not there. Nothing
about the place had changed and none of it meant anything without him in it.
