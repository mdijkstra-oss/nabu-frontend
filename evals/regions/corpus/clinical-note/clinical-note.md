# Outpatient note

Seen in clinic today. Attended alone.

The patient reports that the breathlessness has been worse over the past three
weeks and is now present on one flight of stairs. She describes waking at night
once or twice, and says the ankle swelling is new since the middle of February.

She has been taking the tablets as prescribed and has not missed any.

On examination the chest was clear. Ankles show pitting oedema to mid-calf.
Blood pressure 148/92 at 10:40, repeated at 11:05 as 142/88.

Bloods taken. Echo requested.

My impression is that this is decompensation rather than anything new, and I
have increased the dose accordingly. The patient understood the reason for the
change and agreed with it.

Review in six weeks.
