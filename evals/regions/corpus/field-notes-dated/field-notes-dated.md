# Bellwether Row, site notes

## 3 March 2026

Returned to the site with the moisture meter. Readings on the north wall were
high everywhere above the plate line and normal below it, which points at the
roof rather than the ground.

The caretaker let us in at 09:00 and stayed for the first hour.

We had agreed the scope of this back in January, and it has not changed.

## 4 March 2026

Rain all day. Nothing useful from the exterior, so we worked inside.

At 11:00 the ceiling in the back bedroom was opened for a hand's width. The
felt above it is perished and there is daylight through it in two places.

## 7 March 2026

Dry. Photographs taken from the scaffold, north and east elevations.

The two properties left out of the February survey were added to the schedule.
