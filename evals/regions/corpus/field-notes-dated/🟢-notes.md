# field-notes-dated

Field notes. Dated headings in sequence, times inside each, one passing date mention.

- Person 🟢 — empty. The caretaker is mentioned and owns nothing.
- Date 🟢 — three regions, exactly the three headings, boundaries exact.

All clean. This is the baseline that must not regress when dates start nesting: the three headings should still tile the document, with the times inside them as new nested regions.
